================================================================================
GLOBAL BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

BR-1: Investment > 15 Crore OR project is Tumakuru Machine Tool Park
      → State Level Approval Process (MD → JD → Nodal Officer → Committee)

BR-2: Investment <= 15 Crore
      → District Level Approval Process (JD DIC → DD DIC → AD DIC)

BR-3: Land Requirement > 10 Acres OR (KIADB land source AND land > 2 Acres)
      → Application must go to LAC (Land Audit Committee) meeting

BR-4: Investment 15Cr to 500Cr → SLSWCC meeting
      Investment > 500Cr → HLSWCC meeting

BR-5: For Expansion/Diversification/Modernization applications:
      VFA investment must be at least 25% more than previous investment in VFA

BR-6: Social Category classification:
      All promoters must belong to same social category/gender for social category benefits.
      (SC, ST, Women, Minorities, Backward Classes, Physically Handicapped, Ex-servicemen)

BR-7: Before CAF submission, system verifies DSC registration.
      If not registered, investor redirected to DSC registration page.

BR-8: No payment required for transitioning offline application to online format.

BR-9: Edit Request: Investor can request edit only if application not yet processed by MD.
      Valid justification must be provided.

BR-10: Withdrawal: Investor can withdraw approved CAF by providing proper justification.
       MD approves/rejects withdrawal request.


================================================================================
APPLICATION STATUS FLOW
================================================================================

Submitted → Allocated → Pending With DD (Nodal Officer) → Pending With JD
         → Pending With MD → Approved For Meeting → Meeting Scheduled
         → Approved / Rejected / Deferred

Special Statuses:
- Pending With Investor  (Query raised by DD, approved by MD)
- Processing Offline     (MD marked for offline processing)
- Edit Requested         (Investor requested edit before MD processing)
- Withdrawal Requested   (Investor requested withdrawal)
- Cancelled              (MD cancelled approved CAF)
